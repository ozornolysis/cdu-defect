CDU Defect Finder

1973 Stock. Tap the lights that are lit on the CDU. Answer any follow-up questions to narrow it down. Training aid based on the stock cards. Always follow the Rule Book and Controller instructions.

1. What’s lit on the CDU?

Quick reference: fuses, MCBs, ATOP

20 Amp No 1 Fuse: Traction A, Round Train Circuit, Full Speed Safety Brake, Service Brake Decode Relay, Braking (Motor).

20 Amp No 2 Fuse: Traction D, Slow Speed Safety Circuit, MA Trip, Braking (Trailer).

Blank but pilot light on: 20 Amp Clean Fuse. Blank and no pilot light: 40 Amp Door & Auxiliary Fuse.

Seven Safety Brake MCBs: Round Train, Full Speed, Slow Speed, Service Brake Decode Relay, Braking Motor, Braking Trailer, Braking Control (MCB in UMDM). Emergency brake if Round Train, Full or Slow Speed trips; sluggish if Braking ones trip.

ATOP: A = Air, T = Traction, O = Overloads, P = Pilot light.
